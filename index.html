What Is the FAB Ratibi Card?

The Ratibi card is issued by First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB) and is part of the Wage Protection System (WPS). Employers use it to pay employees who earn up to 5,000 AED per month, though it may also be used for other salary brackets. It works like any standard debit card: you can shop, pay bills, or withdraw cash from ATMs. However, because it is prepaid, you cannot overdraft — you can only spend what is on the card.

This makes checking your balance especially important. Without a regular fab balance check, you might not know if your salary has been credited, or you could accidentally overspend. Let’s explore the most reliable ways to stay on top of your balance.

Main Ways to Check Your FAB Ratibi Balance

You have several options, each suited to different situations:

  • Online portal – free, no app needed, works from any device.
  • SMS – send a text and get an instant reply.
  • ATM – classic, secure, and available 24/7.
  • Phone call – automated voice service or speak to an agent.
  • FAB Mobile App – only if you have a full FAB bank account.
Pro tip: The online portal (Magnati) is the fastest and most convenient way for a fab balance check — no downloads, just a browser and your card number.

How to Check Your FAB Ratibi Balance Online (Step-by-Step)

This is the most recommended method for a quick fab balance check. It works on any smartphone, tablet, or computer. You don’t need to install an app.

1 Visit the official Magnati portal

Open your browser and go to: ppc.magnati.com/ppc-inquiry. Always double‑check you are on the official page to protect your data.

2 Enter your 16‑digit card number

Look at the front of your Ratibi card and carefully type the full number without spaces. Verify it twice before proceeding.

3 Provide verification details

The system will ask for either your Date of Birth or your Emirates ID number. Enter the information exactly as it is linked to your card.

4 Click submit & view your balance

Press the “Go” or “Submit” button. Within seconds, your available balance and recent transactions will appear on the screen. That’s it — no fees, no hassle.

This online method is available 24/7, and it’s completely free. You can do a fab balance check from your office, home, or even while travelling.

Alternative Balance Check Methods

SMS Balance Check (send BAL to 6667)

If you don’t have internet access, SMS is a lifesaver. Open your phone’s messaging app, type BAL in the message body, and send it to 6667. You will receive a reply with your current balance. Make sure your mobile number is registered with your Ratibi card, and note that a small fee (around 0.30 AED) may apply.

ATM Balance Inquiry

ATMs are everywhere, and they offer a secure way to check your balance. Insert your card, enter your 4‑digit PIN, and select “Balance Inquiry”. The screen will show your balance, and you can print a receipt. FAB ATMs are free; other banks may charge a small fee.

Phone Call – FAB Contact Centre

You can call FAB at 600 525 500. Follow the automated voice instructions — you’ll need to enter your 16‑digit card number. The system will read your balance, or you can choose to speak with a representative for further assistance.

What to Do If Your Balance Is Incorrect

Sometimes your balance may not reflect your expected salary. If this happens, don’t panic. First, wait up to 48 hours after payday, as WPS transfers can take a day or two to process. If the delay continues, contact your HR department to confirm the payroll was sent. Finally, if your employer confirms payment, call FAB support at 600 525 500 for investigation.

Important Safety Tips

  • Never share your PIN – keep it secret.
  • Use official channels – only use the Magnati portal, FAB app, or official numbers.
  • Cover the keypad when entering your PIN at an ATM.
  • Check regularly – a routine fab balance check helps you spot unusual activity early.

Understanding the Magnati Portal: A Closer Look

The Magnati portal is the official online gateway for Ratibi cardholders. It is designed to be user‑friendly and works on all major browsers. You don’t need to remember a username or password — just your card number and one verification detail. This makes it one of the simplest ways to perform a fab balance check without any registration.

Many users appreciate that the portal also shows recent transactions. This helps you track where your money went and spot any unauthorized charges quickly. If you see a transaction you don’t recognise, you can immediately contact FAB customer service.

Why Your Salary Might Not Appear Immediately

It’s common to worry when your salary doesn’t show up on payday. However, the Wage Protection System (WPS) involves multiple steps: your employer initiates the transfer, the bank processes it, and then it reaches your Ratibi card. This can take up to 48 hours, especially if payday falls on a weekend or public holiday. Always perform a fab balance check at the end of the working day before assuming there is an issue.

Then, track your spending by checking your balance every few days. This simple habit can help you avoid running out of money before your next salary.

  • Set a daily spending limit – decide how much you can spend each day.
  • Use the transaction history – review where your money goes.
  • Save a small amount – if possible, keep a small buffer for emergencies.

Common Issues and How to Resolve Them

“My card is not working at the ATM”

First, check that you are entering the correct PIN. If it still doesn’t work, your card might be blocked due to multiple incorrect attempts. Call FAB support to unblock it.

“I forgot my PIN”

You cannot retrieve your PIN online. Visit a FAB branch with your Emirates ID and card to reset it.

“The balance on the portal is different from the ATM”

This can happen if there are pending transactions. The online portal shows the most up‑to‑date available balance. If you still see a difference, wait an hour and check again.

Fees and Charges You Should Know

While checking your balance online is free, other services may have fees. ATM withdrawals at non‑FAB machines may cost around 2–5 AED. SMS balance inquiries usually cost 0.30 AED per message. Always check the fee schedule provided with your card to avoid surprises.
